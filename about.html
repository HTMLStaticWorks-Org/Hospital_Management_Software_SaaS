Founded in the ICU & Trauma Ward

We Built MediCore Because Clinicians Deserve Software as Fast as Their Reflexes.

In 2019, our co-founders observed emergency residents spending 42% of their shift re-typing lab orders across four incompatible legacy programs while discharged patients waited hours for manual insurance clearance.

We set out to re-architect the Hospital Information System from first principles—combining sub-second cloud telemetry, native HL7 FHIR R4 interoperability, and human-centered clinical ergonomics.

Mission & Vision

Zero Administrative Friction Between Doctor and Patient.

Our Mission: Give every hospital—from 30-bed regional surgical centers to 2,000-bed academic medical campuses—a unified, self-healing digital nervous system.

Our Vision: A healthcare ecosystem where clinical records travel securely with the patient in real time, medication errors are structurally impossible, and discharge billing settles instantaneously.

Patient Safety First

Every UI screen undergoes clinical cognitive-load testing with practicing ICU nurses and ER physicians to prevent alert fatigue and dosage errors.

Open Interoperability

We reject data lock-in. Every hospital owns 100% of its clinical data with open FHIR R4, HL7 v2.5, and DICOMweb APIs included by default.

Always-On Resilience

Hospitals never close for maintenance. Our architecture guarantees zero-downtime schema upgrades and offline local-LAN continuity.
