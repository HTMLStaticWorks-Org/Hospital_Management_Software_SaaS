10 Deeply Integrated Clinical Modules

Every Department Connected on a Single Clinical Backbone.

Filter by hospital domain below or inspect all 10 end-to-end capabilities—engineered for multi-specialty hospitals, surgical institutes, and outpatient chains.

Filter Modules by Domain

01 · CLINICAL CORE

Longitudinal Patient Records (EMR / EHR)

Give physicians a complete, single-screen timeline of every outpatient visit, inpatient admission, surgical note, allergy alert, and serial lab graph tied to one lifetime UHID.

  • ICD-11 & SNOMED-CT auto-complete clinical problem lists
  • Specialty flowsheets for Cardiology, Oncology, OB-GYN, Pediatrics, and Ortho
  • Real-time drug-drug and drug-allergy contraindication engine
02 · CLINICAL WORKFLOW

OPD & IPD Clinical Workflow Management

Convert an outpatient consultation or emergency triage directly into an inpatient admission with one click—carrying forward all vitals, history, and initial STAT orders automatically.

  • Digital nursing Kardex, intake-output fluid balance charts, and MEWS scoring
  • OT theater booking with WHO Surgical Safety Checklist enforcement
  • Parallel multi-department discharge clearance workflow
03 · OPERATIONS & ADT

Real-Time Bed & Ward Telemetry Board

Eliminate phone calls between admission desks and floor nurses. View live status across ICU, NICU, General Wards, Private Suites, and Emergency resuscitation bays.

  • Color-coded states: Available, Occupied, UV Cleaning, Reserved, Maintenance
  • Automatic room-rent escalation when transferring from General Ward to ICU
  • Housekeeping turnaround SLA timers with mobile attendant alerts
04 · FINANCE & RCM

Automated Billing, Packages & TPA Insurance

Enforce complex corporate and TPA tariff agreements automatically. Track pre-authorization requests, interim enhancements, co-pay splits, and NHCX claim bundles from a single ledger.

  • Automatic package inclusion/exclusion validation for surgeries
  • Credit-limit threshold alerts when running IPD bill exceeds approved pre-auth
  • Integrated UPI QR, Stripe, PayPal, and multi-currency international desk
05 · OPERATIONS & SUPPLY CHAIN

Central Pharmacy & Consignment Inventory

Prevent stock-outs of critical ICU emergency drugs and stop expiry write-offs with automated FEFO (First-Expiry-First-Out) batch allocation and vendor purchase orders.

  • GS1 barcode dispensing linked directly to physician e-prescriptions
  • Narcotic & Schedule-H1 controlled substance register with dual sign-off
  • Cath-lab & orthopedic implant consignment lot tracking
06 · DIAGNOSTICS (LIS / RIS / PACS)

Bi-Directional Laboratory & DICOM PACS

Eliminate manual lab value typing errors. Barcoded sample tubes communicate directly with pathology analyzers, while radiology modalities pull patient demographics via DICOM MWL.

  • Automated Westgard QC charting and critical panic-value SMS escalation
  • Zero-footprint HTML5 DICOM viewer for CT, MRI, CR/DR, and Ultrasound
  • Pathologist digital signature release with QR-verified patient PDF reports
07 · CLINICAL ADMINISTRATION

Staff Shift Roster & Nurse Acuity Scheduling

Balance Morning, Evening, Night, and On-Call shifts across every ward while automatically flagging rest-period violations and medical council license renewals.

  • NABH nurse-to-patient ratio compliance checks (1:1 Ventilated ICU, 1:2 Step-Down)
  • One-click leave approval with automatic cross-cover consultant assignment
  • Automated visiting consultant fee-share calculation
08 · PATIENT ACCESS

Multi-Channel Appointment & Token Engine

Synchronize walk-in reception desks, self-service lobby kiosks, WhatsApp booking bots, and call-center schedules with live lobby TV token displays.

  • Dynamic slot adjustment when a surgeon is called into an emergency OT
  • Automated WhatsApp & SMS appointment reminders with pre-visit instructions
  • Priority triage queue insertion for elderly and acute-care walk-ins
09 · VIRTUAL CARE

HIPAA-Compliant HD Telemedicine Suite

Conduct post-operative follow-ups and chronic-care reviews via encrypted WebRTC video—without forcing patients to install third-party apps.

  • Split-screen charting lets doctors write e-prescriptions while speaking on video
  • Instant payment-link dispatch and home sample collection booking
  • Remote ICU tele-intensivist consult mode for spoke hospitals
10 · EXECUTIVE INTELLIGENCE

Real-Time BI, NABH KPIs & Statutory Reports

Move from end-of-month spreadsheets to live board-ready analytics covering ARPOB, ALOS, OT turnaround, antibiotic stewardship, and TPA aging buckets.

  • 68 pre-configured NABH & JCI clinical quality indicator dashboards
  • Automated IDSP communicable disease & birth/death municipal registries
  • Scheduled PDF/CSV executive digest delivered every morning at 07:00 AM
